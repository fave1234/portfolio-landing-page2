01 — OVERVIEW

The Brief and the season.

Frootify (via its MyBlongo line) launched a limited-edition Ramadan product: nourishing Warm Bowls (healthy pap) designed for Iftar and Suhoor, with complimentary Akara. The goal was to create a vibrant, culturally respectful campaign that drove pre-orders while staying true to Frootify's fresh, energetic brand.

02 — THE CHALLENGE

Three tensions to resolve.

  • Brand FidelityTranslate Frootify's bright, modern health-and-wellness identity into a Ramadan context without losing brand recognition.
  • Conversion on MobileDesign a high-converting pre-order experience that worked seamlessly on mobile (primary traffic) and desktop.
  • Clarity at SpeedCommunicate product contents, heritage, and convenience quickly to busy families.
03 — MY APPROACH & ART DIRECTION

Same brand, new season.

I kept the core Frootify green and orange system intact, then layered authentic Ramadan atmosphere: golden lanterns, soft crescent motifs, warm evening lighting, and subtle pattern work. This created an immediate seasonal feeling while the bold typography and product photography stayed 100% on-brand.

I designed the full digital funnel end-to-end: hero landing page, product explainer sections, ingredient breakdowns, pre-order form (individual + bulk), and supporting social/email headers.
