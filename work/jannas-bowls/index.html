01 — OVERVIEW

The Brand Before.

Janna's Bowls is a fast-growing culinary brand based in Lagos, specializing in rich, authentic Nigerian dishes, smoky jollof rice, and food bowls. Formerly operating as "Janna's Treats", the brand had outgrown its original identity and needed a modern, premium visual system capable of supporting future business expansion.

02 — THE CHALLENGE

Three problems holding it back.

  • Category LimitationThe name "Treats" communicated confectionery and pastries, confusing customers looking for full meals and traditional soups.
  • Visual ClutterThe old emblem combined a gradient badge, fork illustration, and script font, making it illegible at smaller scales.
  • Scalability BottleneckThe visual identity was rigid and could not stretch to accommodate new sub-brands like catering, food sauces, or a culinary academy.
03 — STRATEGY & NAMING

From Treats to Bowls.

We transitioned the brand from "Janna's Treats" to "Janna's Bowls" to immediately reflect its core offering while retaining brand equity. To prepare for long-term growth, I designed a wordmark-driven master brand. By choosing a custom, fluid script paired with clean sans-serif secondary typography, the brand achieves two things:

  • Approachability & WarmthReflecting home-cooked care and hospitality.
  • ScalabilityA clean system that easily adapts across sub-brands using unified badge lockups and a custom "J's" monogram for small-space applications.
